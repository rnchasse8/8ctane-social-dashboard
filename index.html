Growth Playbook

How to grow each account — read from this week's numbers

Transformation stories are the growth engine

The Roche 70→89 mph post was #1 on Instagram (150 likes), #1 on Threads (77 of 153 weekly views) and #1 on TikTok (2,507 views). On YouTube, “+10 mph in 1 year” pulled 4,085 views, over half that platform's entire prior week. The formula: named athlete + measured before/after + timeframe.

The leak is conversion, not reach

51% of Instagram views and 88% of Threads views come from non-followers. Instagram profile visits rose +5.1% and bio link taps +60%, yet Instagram netted −2 followers and YouTube −4. People watch and visit; they just don't follow. No pinned proof post and no follow hook is the likely gap.

Instagram

@8ctanebaseball · 17,970 followers · net −2 this week
What the data says

Reach 11,358 (−10%), views 37,529 (−26%), interactions 625 (−45.6%), but profile visits 371 (+5.1%) and bio link taps 8 (+60%). Reels averaged 91.2 likes per post, up from 89.0 last week; the only photo post in two weeks got 8 likes. Zero carousels published in two weeks. Stories were posted on only 1 of 7 days. 51.4% of views came from non-followers. Funnel: 11,358 reached → 371 profile visits (3.3%) → −2 net followers.

Do this

Keep the all-Reels feed diet. Pin the Roche transformation reel to the top of the profile. Test 2 carousels this week built from before/after velocity data cards. Post Stories at least 4 days a week (Fun Fact series). End captions with a follow hook tied to a series, e.g. “Follow for part 2.”

Stop doing this

Standalone single photo posts (8 likes in two weeks). Posting transformation content as one-offs with no series or follow hook.

This week's #1 move

Pin the Roche reel and launch the transformation series with the next athlete's before/after story.

Threads

@8ctanebaseball · 619 followers · net +5 (best growth rate, +0.81%)
What the data says

Views 153, interactions 1. The Roche post alone drew 77 views, half the week's total. 88.2% of views came from non-followers. Zero replies, quotes and reposts in two weeks.

Do this

Reply to every comment and mention the same day. Post one native text take per day: a real training number plus a one-line opinion. Quote-post Instagram transformation content with a fresh angle instead of copy-pasting it.

Stop doing this

Treating Threads as a repost pipe for Instagram content.

This week's #1 move

Start one conversation thread per day off a real number from the training floor.

YouTube

@8ctaneBaseball · 7,155 subscribers · net −4 this week
What the data says

Views 5,842 (−25.3%). “+10 mph in 1 year” (Oct 9) drew 4,085 views, over half of last week's 7,816 total. This week's “70mph - 89mph in two years” already has 1,103 views on its first day. A generic concept Short drew only 328 views. Zero comments two weeks straight. Net likes were −139 this week: older videos are losing likes faster than new ones gain them.

Do this

Make every Short a transformation or a number. Put a question on screen in the last 2 seconds to break the zero-comment streak. Use the title formula “X mph → Y mph in Z” for transformation Shorts.

Stop doing this

Generic drill or concept Shorts with no measured result attached.

This week's #1 move

Ship one transformation Short using the same title formula as the 4,085-view video.

TikTok

@8ctanebaseball · 10,011 followers · net +4 (steadiest platform)
What the data says

Views ~15,700 (−1.7%), the steadiest numbers of any account. The Roche video was #1 at 2,507 views. Re-running the “offseason seriously” concept decayed from 5,283 views to 1,414. The account went silent 3 of 7 days (Oct 14, 18, 19). Profile views fell to 137 (−17%) while views held up: the same conversion leak as Instagram.

Do this

Post daily and fill the gaps. Open every video with the number or result in the first second. Pin the best transformation video to the profile.

Stop doing this

Re-running the same concept twice; it decays hard.

This week's #1 move

Run a 7-day posting streak, transformation-first.
