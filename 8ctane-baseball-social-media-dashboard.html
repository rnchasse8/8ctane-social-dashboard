What's working

Short-form video is the entire engine on all four platforms — every feed post published this week was video. The single biggest new-post performer was YouTube's Oct 17 hitting-program Short (1,429 views), with TikTok's Oct 17 video right behind it (1,394 views). Last week's standouts were video too, and bigger: YouTube's "+10 mph in 1 year" pulled 4,085 views and TikTok's Oct 11 video 5,283. On Instagram, Reels were 100% of the feed and hold the top post (Coaching Portal reel, Oct 16, 131 likes); the only photo post pulled 7 likes. Threads posted more (5 vs 3) but views per post fell from 51.7 to 17.4.
